# Avoid, Shift, Improve: An Approach to Transport Decarbonisation and Its Application to the Veneto Region

<badge color="warning">Work in Progress – 2026/2027</badge>

## Project Overview

This bachelor's thesis investigates strategies for reducing greenhouse gas emissions from the transport sector through the **Avoid, Shift, Improve (ASI)** framework, applying the approach to the regional transport context of Veneto, Italy.

The research examines how reducing unnecessary travel demand (Avoid), promoting more sustainable transport modes (Shift) and improving vehicle and transport-system efficiency (Improve) can contribute to transport decarbonisation.

## Research Objectives

- Examine the theoretical foundations and policy implications of the ASI framework.
- Evaluate strategies for reducing transport-related greenhouse gas emissions.
- Investigate opportunities for modal shift towards public transport, cycling and walking.
- Assess the applicability of transport decarbonisation strategies to the Veneto region.

## Methodology

The research combines a literature review, analysis of transport policies and quantitative assessment of emission-reduction strategies, using available regional and national data.

## Scope

The study focuses on passenger and freight transport decarbonisation, considering the environmental implications of different mobility strategies and their potential application at regional scale.
